1.Who is responsible for your data

AI Pro Pass, operating at aipropass.online, is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at support@aipropass.online or on WhatsApp at +44 7378 106036.

2.What data we collect

Data you give us

  • Order details: your full name, email address, WhatsApp number, country, and any note you add in the order form.
  • Support messages: what you write to us by email or WhatsApp, including screenshots you send.

Data from payments

  • our payment provider processes your payment. We receive limited information such as your name, email, amount, date, payment status, country and the last digits or type of your card. We do not receive or store your full card number.

Data collected automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, pages visited and the time of your visit, recorded by our hosting provider in server logs.
  • If we use analytics or advertising tools (for example Meta Pixel or Google Analytics), they may set cookies or similar technologies. See section 7.

Data we never collect

  • Your Google account password or login codes. You redeem your link yourself on your own account.

3.Why we use your data

  • To process and deliver your order, send your activation link and bonuses (performance of a contract).
  • To provide customer support, handle replacements and refunds (performance of a contract).
  • To prevent fraud and handle payment disputes and chargebacks (legitimate interest).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as accounting and tax records (legal obligation).
  • To improve the Site and measure our advertising (legitimate interest, or your consent where required).
  • To send you offers, only if you agreed. You can unsubscribe at any time (consent).

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, and only as needed:

  • analytics and advertising providers, if used (see section 7);

5.International transfers

Some of our providers are located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. When we transfer data abroad, we use providers that offer appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, as required by applicable law.

To use these rights, email support@aipropass.online. We reply within 30 days.
